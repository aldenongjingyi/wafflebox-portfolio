About

A studio built on a simple love for photography.

Founded in 2025, WaffleBox started because picking up a camera and learning new techniques never gets old for us, and we wanted somewhere to put that energy.

We also noticed a gap. Other than asking a friend for help, people looking for photos are usually stuck choosing between sketchy shops with no track record, or professional studios that are too pricey or formal. Most people just want their photos done right without any of the extra fluff.

We're remote-first because we don't want to be tied down to one place, and it keeps things lean too — the budget goes toward the shoot instead of rent.

We've worked with food brands, health products, university clubs, and everything in between, same care every time.
